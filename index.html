Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a Business Name and a Limited Liability Company (LTD)?

A Business Name is ideal for sole proprietors or small trades where the business owner and the business are seen as the same legal entity. A Limited Liability Company (LTD) treats the company as a completely separate legal entity, meaning your personal properties are fully protected if the business faces liabilities or debts.

What documents do I need to provide for registration?

For a basic registration, you will only need to provide two proposed business names, your valid means of government identification (e.g., NIN, Voter's Card, International Passport, or Driver's License), your passport photograph, signature scan, and basic business details.

Will I get a Tax Identification Number (TIN) automatically?

Yes! Under current regulations, once your Limited Liability Company (LTD) registration is fully approved by the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC), your corporate Tax Identification Number (TIN) is generated automatically and printed right on your certificate.

What happens if my proposed business names are rejected?

If the CAC rejects your initial choices because the names are too similar to an existing brand or contain prohibited words, don't worry! We will work closely with you to submit fresh alternatives immediately at no extra name-search cost to you.
